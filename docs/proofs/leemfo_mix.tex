\title{A mixed TCC--Born inverse of LEEM Fourier optics:\\
algorithm selection}
\author{Wilson Lau}
\date{26 August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We invert a two-dimensional through-focal LEEM intensity stack for the
exit wave $\psi_0=\sigma\,\ee^{\ii\phi}$ against the bilinear
Fourier-optics (FO) kernel of Yu, Lau and Altman~\cite{Yu2019}.
Among discrete inverses that are identities of that kernel, the
cheapest large-phase estimator at modelled $N=128$ is a mixed
transmission-cross-coefficient (TCC)--Born homotopy: vacuum
$2\times 2$ Tikhonov initialization, rank-$M$ TCC (or exact rank-1
under perfect coherence) apply, remainder-corrected Born updates on
the same Fourier-diagonal $2\times 2$, per-bin skip
$t\in\{0,1\}$, and optional algebraic Armijo on the exact quartic line
energy.
This note records \emph{every} candidate examined against that mix,
with a Pick / Component / Not-pick verdict and the Lean witness or
an explicit informal tag.
The scientific inverse statement is the companion
note~\cite{Lau2026inverse}; algebraic claims are machine-checked in
Lean~4.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem and selection criteria}
\label{sec:criteria}

The forward map is bilinear FO intensity~\cite{Yu2019}
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:I}
I(\mathbf{r},\Dz)
=\iint
\boldsymbol{\Psi}(\mathbf{q})\,\boldsymbol{\Psi}^*(\mathbf{q}')
\,\RFO(\mathbf{q},\mathbf{q}',\Dz)
\,\ee^{2\pi\ii(\mathbf{q}-\mathbf{q}')\cdot\mathbf{r}}
\,\dd^2q\,\dd^2q',
\end{equation}
with $\boldsymbol{\Psi}=\mathcal{F}[\psi_0]$ and kernel support
$\abs{\mathbf{q}},\abs{\mathbf{q}'}\le\qap$.
On a finite frequency group $G$ and a finite defocus index $\kappa$,
the same bilinear form is \texttt{ihat} on the 2D kernel
\texttt{R\_FO2}~\cite{Lau2026proofs,Lau2026inverse}.

A candidate is a \textbf{Pick} for 2D large $\phi$ only if
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item it is an \emph{identity} of \texttt{ihat}/\texttt{R\_FO2}
(FO-faithful), and
\item it is strictly cheaper than a dense pair apply $KN^2$ at
$N=128$ in the cost model $\mathrm{dftCost}(N):=N\log_2 N$
(\texttt{dftCost} is a definition, not an FFT existence theorem).
\end{enumerate}
A method may be a \textbf{Component} of the winner without being a
standalone 2D inverse.
\textbf{Not pick} means it fails faithfulness, cost, or the experiment
geometry (through-focal LEEM with known aberration $\chi$).
Convergence rates, statistical noise models and Banach fixed-point
theorems are not FO identities and are tagged \emph{informal}.

\section{Winner mix}
\label{sec:winner}

The locked 2D large-phase estimator is
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Init.} Vacuum $2\times 2$ Tikhonov on $\RFO$ in 2D
(\texttt{stage1Pair2}/\texttt{tikhonovXhat2}), default $K=2$
independent defoci.
\item \textbf{Apply.} Rank-$M$ TCC (\texttt{ihat\_tcc}) or exact
rank-1 autocorrelation under \texttt{PerfectCoherence}.
Rank policy \texttt{recommendTccRank}: $M=1$ if perfectly coherent
or if using the quartic line search, else $M\le 8$ at $N=128$.
\item \textbf{Update.} Remainder-corrected Born
(\texttt{mixedBinStep}) on the same Fourier-diagonal $2\times 2$.
\item \textbf{Skip.} Per-bin \texttt{remainderWeight}${}\in\{0,1\}$
(\texttt{mixed2DMix}): quiet $t=0$ keeps stage~1; loud $t=1$ takes
the full FO remainder.
\item \textbf{Line search (optional).} Algebraic Armijo
(\texttt{lineDescentStep}) on stack fidelity \texttt{lineFid}.
\item \textbf{Partner.} Hermitian data and odd $qmap$ recover
$\delta(-\xi)=\conj{v(\xi)}$ (\texttt{mixed2D\_conj\_partner}).
\end{enumerate}
FO-faithfulness is the exact quadratic homotopy
(\texttt{ihat\_homotopy\_cubic\_zero}): there is no cubic remainder.
Cost: a rank-$M\le 8$ Born step is cheaper than dense at $N=128$
(\texttt{bornStepCost\_lt\_dense\_128},
\texttt{hybridCost\_lt\_dense\_succ}).
The one-dimensional sinusoid of Yu~\emph{et al.} remains Jacobi--Anger
(\texttt{sinusoidJ}), not a branch of \texttt{mixed2D}.

Quiet skip is the linearized / weak-object branch
(\texttt{mixedSpectrumMix\_skip\_step}); there is no separate
weak-phase inverse.
\texttt{mixed2D} with free schedules $(t,\mathrm{damp})$ stays the
general pair API; \texttt{mixed2DMix} is the recommended skip policy.

\section{Picked components}
\label{sec:pick}

\paragraph{Vacuum $2\times 2$ Tikhonov / Schiske--Wiener.}
\textbf{Pick as init} and as the quiet-bin map.
For $\alpha>0$, \texttt{tikhonovXhat2} is the unique minimizer of the
bin energy \texttt{tikhonovJ2} (\texttt{tikhonov2\_unique}).
One Gauss--Newton step from vacuum \emph{is} that solve
(\texttt{vacuumGN\_eq\_stage1Pair2}).
Default $K=2$: $K=1$ always has a kernel on
$(X(\mathbf{q}),\conj{X(-\mathbf{q})})$
(\texttt{exists\_one\_defocus\_pair\_kernel}).
Closed-form Cramer, not an iterative $2\times 2$ solver.

\paragraph{Rank-$M$ TCC apply.}
\textbf{Pick as apply.} Hopkins expansion \texttt{ihat\_tcc} with
modelled cost $KM\cdot 2\cdot N\log_2 N$.
At $N=128$, $M\le 8$ is cheaper than dense
(\texttt{tccApplyCost\_lt\_dense\_128}); $M=9$ is still cheaper
(\texttt{tccApplyCost\_lt\_dense\_128\_nine}); $M\ge 10$ is not.
The Born cap stays $M\le 8$.
Zero-weight padding of unused modes is free
(\texttt{tccKernel\_insert\_weight\_zero}).

\paragraph{Perfect-coherence rank-1 autocorrelation.}
\textbf{Pick when \texttt{PerfectCoherence}} ($q_{\mathrm{ill}}=\Delta E=0$):
\texttt{ihat\_R\_FO\_of\_perfect} / \texttt{ihat\_rank1}.
Not picked from ``envelope $\approx 1$'': \texttt{exists\_R\_FO\_ne\_R0}
on a column with $\Delta E=0$ but $q_{\mathrm{ill}}\neq 0$.

\paragraph{Remainder-corrected Born / quadratic homotopy.}
\textbf{Pick as update.} The true ray is
$\hat I(\mathrm{vac}+t\delta)=\hat I(\mathrm{vac})+t\,DI+t^2\hat I(\delta)$.
\texttt{bornModel} interpolates stage-1 ($t=0$) and full FO ($t=1$).
Loud bins use $t=1$; quiet bins use $t=0$.

\paragraph{Per-bin skip \texttt{remainderWeight}.}
\textbf{Pick as skip.} The control is a bit
(\texttt{remainderWeight\_eq\_zero\_or\_one}).
Global $t$ and fractional $t=\min(1,\norm{\hat I}/\eta)$ are not the
mix (\cref{sec:fail}).

\paragraph{Algebraic Armijo on the exact quartic.}
\textbf{Pick as optional 1D mix} after a Born direction.
Along $x_0+s\cdot d$ the residual is degree 2, so stack fidelity
\texttt{lineFid} is a real quartic (\texttt{lineFid\_quadEnergy}).
\texttt{descentStep} decreases energy when the cubic slope at $0$ is
negative (\texttt{lineFid\_descent}).
Full NLS on the ray is the companion \texttt{nlsJ\_line}; the FO 1D
mix stays fidelity-only because bins already use $\alpha$ in
\texttt{mixedBinStep}.

\paragraph{Hermitian partner / odd \texttt{qmap}.}
\textbf{Pick as recovery.} Hypotheses are
$q(-\xi)=-q(\xi)$ and Hermitian data, not odd $\abs{G}$.

\paragraph{Jacobi--Anger for a 1D sinusoid.}
\textbf{Pick as a 1D special case} (\texttt{sinusoidJ},
\texttt{stage2Sinusoid}).
\textbf{Not pick as a 2D inverse}: the Bessel ladder has $M$ modes,
bilinear FO has $M^2$ pairs
(\texttt{exists\_modeSet\_lt\_modePairs}).

\paragraph{LR+D apply.}
\textbf{Optional component}, not the default apply.
Tighter truncation bound than plain TCC, strictly costlier
(\texttt{tccApplyCost\_lt\_lrPlusDiagApplyCost}); still cheaper than
dense at the Born cap.

\section{Not pick: FO-map mismatch}
\label{sec:fail}

\paragraph{Transport of intensity / Fresnel multiplier.}
Wrong PDE; the multiplier is nonzero outside the aperture where FO is
blind (\texttt{exists\_interior\_R\_FO\_ne\_tie},
\texttt{exists\_interior\_R\_FO2\_ne\_tie},
\texttt{R\_FO\_ne\_tie\_outside}).

\paragraph{Gerchberg--Saxton / HIO / error reduction / $\RCTF$.}
The coherent intensity is
$\RCTF=\RFO\,\Gamma_C\Gamma_S$; $\Gamma_S\neq 1$ off axis
(\texttt{exists\_R\_CTF\_ne\_R\_FO}).
HIO/ER use the same misspecified projector
(\texttt{exists\_hio\_kernel\_ne\_R\_FO}).

\paragraph{Coherent projector $R_0$ as FO.}
Partial spatial coherence: \texttt{exists\_R\_FO\_ne\_R0}.

\paragraph{Single-defocus Wiener.}
The vacuum $2\times 2$ always has a kernel
(\texttt{exists\_one\_defocus\_pair\_kernel}).

\paragraph{Weak-phase CTF $\sin(2\pi\chi_S)$ as the map.}
Can vanish while the FO slice does not
(\texttt{exists\_weakPhase\_sin\_zero\_R\_FO\_ne\_zero}).
Quiet Mix skip is the linearized path; this sine is not that path.

\paragraph{Radial / $\norm{\mathbf{q}}$-only 2D kernel.}
$E_S$ contains $\mathbf{q}\cdot\mathbf{q}'$
(\texttt{exists\_R\_FO2\_ne\_R\_FO\_of\_norms},
\texttt{exists\_aS2\_ne\_radial\_aS}).
Cartesian \texttt{R\_FO2} stays the 2D kernel.

\paragraph{Jacobi--Anger as a 2D inverse / Bool dispatcher on \texttt{mixed2D}.}
\texttt{exists\_modeSet\_lt\_modePairs}. Types differ: scalar $\phi$-cost
versus Fourier-bin $2\times 2$.

\paragraph{First-order Rytov increment.}
$i\phi$ is not $\ee^{i\phi}-1$ on FO
(\texttt{exists\_rytov\_inc\_ne\_fo\_phase}).

\paragraph{Forgetful Kaczmarz.}
Last-row only; not the \texttt{tikhonovJ2} minimizer for $K\ge 2$
(\texttt{exists\_kaczmarzSweep\_ne\_tikhonovXhat2}) and costlier than
one Cramer solve (\texttt{binSolveCost\_lt\_kaczmarzCost\_one\_sweep}).

\paragraph{Unit Gauss--Newton as a line critical point.}
The exact cubic at a unit GN step ($B=-A$) is not identically zero
(\texttt{lineCubic\_exactGN}); a unit step can raise energy
(\texttt{exists\_unit\_step\_energy\_increase}).

\paragraph{Gauss--Newton Hessian as the quartic Hessian.}
Drops the remainder coupling $2\operatorname{Re}(\conj{A}C)$
(\texttt{exists\_quadA2\_ne\_gn}).

\paragraph{Quadratic Newton candidate $-A_1/(2A_2)$.}
Ignores $A_3,A_4$; not a cubic root; can raise energy
(\texttt{exists\_newtonCandidate\_not\_critical},
\texttt{exists\_newtonCandidate\_energy\_increase}).

\paragraph{Global or fractional homotopy $t$ as skip.}
Exact FO is loud $t=1$; stage-1 is quiet $t=0$.
Distinct $t$ disagree on occupied remainder
(\texttt{exists\_bornModel\_t\_disagree});
loud $t=0$ drops the remainder
(\texttt{bornRhs\_t0\_ignores\_loud}).
Fractional $t=\min(1,\norm{\hat I}/\eta)$ is neither endpoint.

\section{Not pick: faithful or equivalent, but not the cheap mix}
\label{sec:cost}

\paragraph{Dense / generic-background Gauss--Newton.}
FO-faithful (exact quadratic remainder) but \texttt{ihatJac} off vacuum
is not Fourier-diagonal and is $KN^2$, not $O(KN\log N)$.

\paragraph{Real-space Born--GN as the cheap mix.}
Same obstruction as dense GN.

\paragraph{Always-on quartic line search.}
Faithful 1D mix, but \texttt{recommendTccCap} then forces $M=1$.
Rank-1 line search beats dense at $N=128$
(\texttt{lineSearchCost\_lt\_dense\_128\_rank1}); rank-8 line search
does not.
Born $M\le 8$ is the cheaper 2D default; Armijo stays optional.

\paragraph{Tikhonov line replacing \texttt{lineFid}.}
$\texttt{nlsJ}=\texttt{lineFid}+\alpha\sum\norm{x_0+sd}^2$
(\texttt{nlsJ\_line}).
Bins already use $\alpha$.
Fidelity-only \texttt{lineFid} can be flat while
$\norm{x_0+sd}$ is unbounded
(\texttt{exists\_lineFid\_flat\_unbounded}).

\paragraph{Two-source special case in \texttt{recommendTccRank}.}
\texttt{ihat\_twoSource} is \texttt{ihat\_tcc} at $M=2$.

\paragraph{$\Delta E=0$ as rank-1 apply.}
Chromatic envelope $1$ is not \texttt{PerfectCoherence}
(\texttt{exists\_R\_FO\_ne\_R0} on \texttt{nacPC}).

\paragraph{Landweber / CGLS / CG as bin solvers.}
Dimension $2$: exact CG recovers Cramer after the same $O(K)$ Gram
work; rates are informal.
Stage-1 stays closed-form \texttt{tikhonovXhat2}.

\paragraph{Recursive least squares / Kalman.}
Proper accumulation of the Gram is the same estimator as batch Cramer,
not cheaper.
Forgetful streaming is Kaczmarz (already rejected).

\paragraph{Three-point $\{0,\texttt{descentStep},1\}$ min.}
Same \texttt{lineSearchCost} as Armijo; not required (T15 already has
descent).
Fixed $\{0,\tfrac12,1\}$ does not guarantee descent.

\paragraph{LR+D as the default apply.}
See \cref{sec:pick}: optional tighter bound, not cheaper.

\paragraph{Near-perfect coherence as hard $M=1$.}
Rank-1 apply needs \texttt{PerfectCoherence}.
Near-PC belongs under \texttt{ihat\_tcc\_trunc\_bound} /
\texttt{chooseTccRank}, not a hard cap of $1$.

\paragraph{Formal $K>2$ always better / iterate count $T=1$ because quadratic.}
$K=2$ is the algebraic default; extra $K$ is SNR / common CTF zeros
(informal).
Quadratic FO is the \emph{model}, not one-step exactness of Born;
$T$ stays API (\texttt{hybridCost} is parametric in all $T$).

\section{Not pick: not an FO identity, or a different experiment}
\label{sec:informal}

Each item below was examined and left informal or out of geometry.
No new \texttt{exists\_*} is claimed.

\paragraph{Cardano roots of \texttt{lineCubic}.}
Stationarity is a cubic; roots are not constructed.
Armijo is the encoded 1D mix.

\paragraph{Trust-region / dogleg / cubic regularization.}
The quadratic model is not the FO quartic; a cubic model returns to
Cardano.

\paragraph{Complex-step line search.}
A complex step is sesquilinear in $(\sigma,\conj{\sigma})$, not
\texttt{quadPoly}. Real $s\in\R$ only.

\paragraph{Nesterov / FISTA / Polyak / Heavy-ball.}
Acceleration rates, not FO identities; same bucket as GN/Born
convergence.

\paragraph{L-BFGS / BFGS / Levenberg--Marquardt.}
Secant / damped-GN models inherit the GN Hessian gap
(\texttt{exists\_quadA2\_ne\_gn}) and lose the Fourier-diagonal
$2\times 2$ Born loop.

\paragraph{Anderson / DIIS mixing.}
Multi-history residual LS, not the scalar damp of
\texttt{mixedBinStep}; not cheaper per outer step.

\paragraph{Wirtinger / ADMM / PhaseLift convergence.}
Not FO identities.
The PC kernel mismatch is already \texttt{exists\_R\_FO\_ne\_R0}.

\paragraph{Banach FPT / Picard iterate convergence.}
\texttt{picard\_unique\_of\_lip} is only a Lipschitz implication.
Existence and rates are not encoded.

\paragraph{TV / Split-Bregman / $\ell^1$.}
Drops the Cramer uniqueness story of $\alpha\norm{\cdot}^2$.
FO-faithfulness of the mix comes from the quadratic homotopy, not the
penalty.

\paragraph{Weighted LS / VST / $1/\abs{h}^2$.}
Statistical; \texttt{tikhonovJ2}/\texttt{nlsJ} are unweighted
$\ell^2$. No probability space.

\paragraph{Huber / truncated least squares.}
A robust $\rho(r)$ breaks $\norm{\texttt{quadPoly}}^2\to$ quartic, so
it is not the FO 1D mix.

\paragraph{Richardson--Lucy / Poisson EM.}
No probability space; not bilinear FO.

\paragraph{MAL / phase diversity as the large-$\phi$ inverse.}
GN stacked over extra defoci when $\chi$ is \emph{unknown}~\cite{Coene1996}.
The mix assumes known $\chi$ in \texttt{R\_FO2} and is cheaper.

\paragraph{Ptychography / Wigner-distribution deconvolution.}
Probe--diffraction geometry, not a through-focal LEEM stack.

\paragraph{Multi-slice / dynamical scattering.}
A volume propagator, not bilinear \texttt{ihat}.

\paragraph{Learned / unrolled neural inverse.}
Not an FO identity.

\paragraph{Image alignment / drift as the inverse.}
Preprocessing. Inverse inputs are already Fourier bins.
Alignment and the current-to-defocus map remain informal in the
inverse note.

\paragraph{Contrast-normalized $y/\langle I\rangle$ or $y_k-y_0$.}
Experiment convention.
The mix uses raw $y$ and vacuum-subtracted \texttt{yLin}.

\paragraph{Defocus interpolant on $\Dz$.}
$\kappa$ is given; missing frames shrink the index.
Uneven $\Dz$ is already an arbitrary map $\kappa\to\R$.

\paragraph{Energy-filtered / spectroscopic stack as $\kappa$.}
The inverse index is defocus, not energy.
Bibliographic energy-filtered LEEM is not this mix.

\paragraph{SPLEEM / magnetic contrast.}
Scalar \texttt{objectWave} $\sigma\,\ee^{i\phi}$; spin is a different
channel.

\paragraph{Pin $\operatorname{Im}(\delta 0)=0$ for uniqueness.}
\texttt{ihat\_gauge}: global phase leaves intensity and
$\sum\norm{\Psi}^2$ invariant. Gauge theatre.

\paragraph{Deprecate \texttt{mixed2D}; restrict to \texttt{modeSet}; DC-bin special case.}
\texttt{mixed2D} stays the general pair API.
\texttt{modeSet} is the 1D JA ladder; outside-disk bins already vanish
(T4).
DC uses $\texttt{yLin}=y-\RFO(0,0)$; partner at $0$ is $v=\conj{u}$.

\section{Not claimed}
\label{sec:not}

FFT existence or wall-clock $O(N\log N)$; array DFT $=$ continuum FT;
Gaussian/Poisson/MSE/Cram\'er--Rao; frozen-aperture and first-order
tilt truncations already flagged \texttt{Approx.} in the forward
model; local quadratic convergence of GN/Born; ``fastest among all
conceivable inverses'' (only DFT-based / dense-bilinear / TCC
comparisons are proved); uniqueness of the nonlinear inverse
(\texttt{ihat\_gauge}).
